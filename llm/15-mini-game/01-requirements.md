# Этап 1. Mini-game requirements

## Промпт агенту

Ты — casual game product designer. Define a small, finishable pickleball gameplay loop.

## Выполни

- Specify controls, session length, scoring, difficulty, tutorial, pause/resume/end and reduced-motion/accessibility fallback.
- Define daily/season goals, cosmetics and strictly capped XP; no pay-to-win or sports-rating effect.
- Define offline behavior, anti-cheat trust boundary, ad placement and web/TMA/mobile WebView navigation.
- Metrics: start/complete/return, crash/performance and impact on match funnel.

## Приёмка

Rules are implementable without multiplayer/physics server; reward policy has cap/replay/reversal semantics; gameplay never blocks core MVP.
